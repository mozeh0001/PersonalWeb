MR AngryBooks

Unity | C# | Mixed Reality | Meta Quest


MR AngryBooks is a mixed reality building and physics-based gameplay experience where players construct a virtual environment using real-world surfaces before transitioning into a slingshot-based gameplay phase.


Players can place and modify virtual objects throughout their physical environment, then use the space they created as the arena for eliminating enemies.





  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Focus: Gameplay Systems, Enemy Spawning, UI, VFX & Integration
  • Platform: Meta Quest
  • Engine: Unity

My Contributions


I worked primarily on the core gameplay systems that transition the experience from its mixed reality building phase into a complete physics-based gameplay loop.


  • Core Gameplay Loop: Implemented the main play phase and progression logic.
  • Enemy Spawning: Developed enemy spawning and tracking during gameplay with stable spatial anchoring and placement on detected tables, floors, and other surfaces.
  • System Integration: Integrated gameplay systems with the object placement system to support the transition between building and playing.
  • Gameplay UI: Developed UI for shot count, enemy tracking, and end-state feedback.
  • Visual Feedback: Implemented visual effects for enemy deaths and projectile collisions.

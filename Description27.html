Octopus Anti-Spilling Coffee Coaster with Sound Module

Fusion 360 | Mechanical Design | CAD | Electronics


A mechanical coffee coaster designed in Fusion 360 that uses articulated octopus tentacles to react when a cup is placed on the platform.


The design combines mechanical joints with a small sound module, adding an electronic response to the physical interaction.





Project Showcase

Mechanism & Electronics


  • Uses a sound module that plays a different recorded sound when the cup presses down.
  • Uses a revolute joint at each elbow so the tentacles can rotate.
  • Includes a prismatic joint inside the wheel where inner sections slide vertically.
  • Couples the prismatic motion with elbow rotation to raise the tentacles around the cup.
  • Uses a gravity-resetting hinge in the head so the mechanism returns to its resting pose when the cup is removed.

Technical Drawings for Manufacture


Individual CAD components and technical drawings were prepared for fabrication and assembly.

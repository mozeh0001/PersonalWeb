Octopus Anti-Spilling Coffee Coaster with Sound Module

Made Using Fusion 360



  • Uses unique sounds from the module each time a cup is placed on the coaster.
  • Sound module is a small self-contained electronic board with a built-in speaker, battery slot, and push-button trigger that plays a recorded audio clip when activated.
  • Uses a revolute joint at each elbow, allowing the arms to swing when a cup presses down.
  • Includes a true prismatic joint inside the wheel where the inner elbow sections slide vertically like a small piston.
  • The prismatic motion drives the elbow rotation, creating a coupled mechanism that lifts the tentacles.
  • The head is mounted on a simple gravity-resetting hinge, returning to its resting pose when the weight is removed.

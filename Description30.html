Multiplayer Networking

HaRVI Lab: Social Touch Research

Unity | BlazePose | OpenCV | AR Interaction | Hardware Integration | Multiplayer (2 users)

This project was developed as part of research with the Haptics Robotics and Virtual Interaction (HaRVI) Laboratory at USC. The goal of the work is to explore how touch interactions can be shared between people through digital systems.

Using computer vision and pose detection, gestures such as taps, presses, and hand movements are tracked and translated into virtual touch events inside a shared environment.





  • Gesture Tracking: BlazePose and OpenCV detect body and hand motion.
  • AR Interaction: Users interact with virtual objects and spaces in real time.
  • Multiplayer Social Touch: Multiple users can join the same session, allowing touch gestures and Unity collision events to trigger shared haptic feedback between participants using actuators in hardware.
  • Remote Communication: The system explores how touch can be integrated into video calls and AR experiences.

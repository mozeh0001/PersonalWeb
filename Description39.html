Kapture the Snake of 88'

Unity | C# | Custom Hardware | AI | Multiplayer



Kapture the Snake of 88' is a co-op horror investigation game where two private investigators return to an abandoned college dorm to solve a decades-old murder.


Players use custom SnakeBoy controllers to detect, capture, and communicate with spirits hidden throughout the environment. By interviewing suspects, analyzing testimony, and connecting evidence, players must determine which ghost was responsible for the murders.



  • Role: Lead Game Programmer, Hardware Engineer & Sound Designer
  • Focus: Gameplay Systems, Multiplayer, AI Dialogue, Hardware Integration, PCB Design & Audio
  • Engine: Unity
  • Genre: Co-op Horror Investigation

My Contributions


As Lead Game Programmer, Hardware Engineer, and Sound Designer, I worked across the project's gameplay, physical controller, multiplayer, AI, and audio systems.


  • Gameplay Systems: Developed the investigation flow, ghost interactions, interviews, evidence board, voting, and round progression.
  • AI & Voice: Integrated speech recognition, LLM-powered ghost dialogue, generated voices, and character-specific behaviors.
  • Multiplayer: Developed the two-player investigation architecture and synchronized gameplay systems.
  • Hardware Engineering: Integrated the custom SnakeBoy controllers with Unity, including IMU input, joystick controls, OLED output, LEDs, microphone input, and physical controls.
  • Fabrication: Worked on CAD, PCB development, 3D printing, hardware assembly, and iterative controller prototyping.
  • Sound Design: Created environmental audio, UI feedback, material-based footsteps, and retro horror-inspired audio.

Usability & Playtesting


Because the experience combines a custom physical controller, multiplayer investigation, AI conversation, and traditional gameplay, playtesting was essential for evaluating both usability and player engagement.


Players were especially captivated by interviewing the ghosts, drawing connections between their stories, and piecing together the mystery. Open-ended conversations helped players feel connected to the characters and encouraged them to investigate beyond the information strictly needed to identify the killer.


  • Tested controller comfort and physical input placement
  • Evaluated whether players understood the Kapture interaction and investigation flow
  • Tested OLED readability and hardware feedback during active gameplay
  • Observed how players approached open-ended AI ghost interviews
  • Evaluated how players connected character testimony and used information to reason about the mystery
  • Iterated on hardware and gameplay interactions based on QA and player feedback

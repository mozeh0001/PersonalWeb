About Me

I’m a BS/MS Computer Science student at USC focused on gameplay and hardware engineering, immersive systems, and interactive technologies. My work spans Unreal and Unity development, embedded hardware and IoT, VR/haptics, and AI-driven systems, with a focus on creating experiences that connect physical and digital interaction.

I develop gameplay systems, mechanics, UI, and tools for real-time interactive experiences, while also prototyping custom hardware and IoT devices using C++, Particle, and Arduino. My work with JavaScript, Node.js, Python, machine learning, and NLP allows me to connect these systems with full-stack and AI-driven applications.

I’m especially interested in rapid prototyping, alternative interfaces, and unconventional game mechanics. Outside of engineering, I study jazz saxophone and improvisation, which influences how I approach experimentation and creative problem-solving.

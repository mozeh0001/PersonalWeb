Zoofers Moofers

Personal Project | Game Designer & Programmer | Unity | C# | IoT | Wwise | CAD



Zoofers Moofers is a whimsical toy-testing game inspired by SCP, Willy Wonka and the Chocolate Factory, and Cruelty Squad. Players test strange toys inside Mr. Wondertainment's shop and collect all five toys while discovering their unusual mechanics.


The experience combines traditional Unity gameplay with a custom IoT glove, allowing players to physically grip, squeeze, tilt, and gesture instead of relying entirely on a mouse and keyboard.


The project was independently submitted to the Game Developers Conference Independent Games Festival and was eligible for recognition as a women-led and marginalized-gender-led studio.

IoT Glove Integration


  • Integrated a custom IoT glove using pressure sensors and motion sensing.
  • Added five finger sensors and one wrist sensor for gripping, squeezing, and tapping.
  • Used motion data to detect tilting, lifting, and other 3D hand gestures.
  • Connected physical gestures directly to virtual toy interactions in Unity.
  • Added Glove / No Glove modes so the game remains accessible without the custom hardware.

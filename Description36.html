Call of Wizards

Unity | C# | VR | Meta Quest


Call of Wizards is a wave-based VR combat game where players draw symbols in 3D space to cast elemental spells and fight waves of enemies.





  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Focus: VR Systems, Spellcasting, Enemy AI, UI & Integration
  • Platform: Meta Quest
  • Engine: Unity

My Contributions


I worked primarily on gameplay programming and VR systems, including spellcasting mechanics, enemy combat, wave progression, UI feedback, and overall Unity integration.


  • VR Spellcasting: Gesture recognition, spell combinations, drawing feedback, haptics, and elemental effects
  • Enemy AI: NavMesh movement, chasing, attacks, status effects, health, and death behavior
  • Combat & Wave Progression: Enemy wave system, combat pacing, player-enemy interactions, rewards, and damage feedback
  • VR UI & Feedback: World-space UI, health bars, wave announcements, reward popups, blood effects, and spell feedback
  • Technical Integration: Quest deployment, debugging, optimization, and final gameplay integration

Elemental Spell Combinations


The system supports dual-hand casting, allowing each controller to independently recognize and store an elemental spell. When two elements are prepared within a short timing window, they combine into stronger attacks, healing abilities, or status effects.


  • Circle + Circle: Group Frost
  • Circle + Triangle: Deep Freeze
  • Triangle + Triangle: Area Burn
  • V + Triangle: Shock Burn
  • V + V: Group Stun
  • Circle + V: Heal

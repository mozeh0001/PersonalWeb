DoReMimis

Unreal Engine | Multiplayer | Motion Capture | Mobile Integration


DoReMimis is an interactive experience that blurs the line between film and video games. Players watch a cinematic story while controlling playable side characters in real time from their phones.


Throughout the experience, players participate in collaborative minigames that directly influence the story, with win and loss conditions affecting how the narrative progresses.



Role: Networking Engineer

Engine: Unreal Engine

Project Type: Interactive Film / Multiplayer Game

Development: Team-Based Project

Core Experience


Players take control of Mimis living music notes that have come to life to help Santi work through his creative blockers.


  • Phone-Based Gameplay: Players control their own Mimi from a mobile device while participating in the shared experience.
  • Collaborative Minigames: Players work together during interactive sequences embedded throughout the film.
  • Branching Narrative: Winning or losing minigames can influence how the story progresses.
  • Interactive Cinematics: The experience transitions between cinematic storytelling and real-time gameplay.


Project Pitch Deck

Technology & Production


DoReMimis combines game development and filmmaking techniques to create a shared interactive cinematic experience.


  • Unreal Engine: Powers the real-time gameplay and cinematic environment.
  • Motion Capture: Mocap and MetaHuman workflows are used for human characters and performances.
  • Multiplayer: Multiple players participate simultaneously within the shared experience.
  • Replication: Networked gameplay requires player actions and game state to remain synchronized across clients.
  • Mobile Integration: Players use their phones as individual interfaces while interacting with the shared experience.

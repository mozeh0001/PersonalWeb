Iron Testament

Programmer & Audio Designer

Unity | C# | GarageBand | Wwise | Blender


Iron Testament is a turn-based strategy game developed by Rat Economy, a team of 19 USC Games students, and showcased at the USC Games Expo. Players lead a sect of life-worshipping robots against an adversary AI uprising, using positioning and unique abilities to outmaneuver enemies through tactical, turn-based combat.

Programming

A* Pathfinding Algorithm


  • Worked through weekly and bi-weekly sprints to deliver gameplay features.
  • Implemented A* pathfinding so players can move units to desired positions on the grid.
  • Added a Line Renderer to visualize the route units would follow.
  • Added movement restrictions to prevent units from navigating between disconnected islands.

Raycast Transparency System

  • Implemented transparency for objects blocking the camera's view of a unit.
  • Used raycasts to detect intersections between the camera and environmental objects.
  • Limited the effect based on camera distance and raycast range.

Tile Indicators

  • Created attack and movement patterns around the tactical grid.
  • Implemented Manhattan and Euclidean tile highlighting for ability ranges.
  • Allowed players to preview the effective range of abilities before committing actions.

Gameplay UI

  • Developed health bars that billboard toward the camera.

  • Built an in-game console system for gameplay logs and combat messages.

  • Used Unity Rich Text to visually distinguish different message types.

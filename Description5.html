Venger

Game Design | Level Design | Unity | VFX


Venger is a survival-horror experience set inside fictional 1990s Soviet-style apartment environments, where players must escape mysterious pursuers while navigating intense physical and psychological threats.


I was one of the project's early game design contributors, helping shape the level structure, enemy concepts, environmental challenges, and overall survival experience.



  • Role: Game Designer & Unity Engineer
  • Team: 9-Person Multidisciplinary Team
  • Development: September 2024 – May 2025

Level & Game Design


I helped design environments and enemy encounters around visibility, sound, navigation, and player decision-making rather than traditional combat alone.


  • Designed labyrinthine environments using darkness, light, and sound as gameplay tools.
  • Created level blueprints built around high-tension navigation and survival encounters.
  • Designed enemy concepts that interact differently with the surrounding environment.
  • Structured enemies as puzzles that require observation, quick interpretation, and reaction.
  • Helped shape encounters where shadows could either conceal threats or provide safety.

Unity Engineering


I also contributed directly to the project's Unity implementation by developing a custom visual-effects system for the Echo enemy.


  • Developed a particle-based transformation effect for the Echo enemy.
  • Used VFX Graph and signed distance field deformation to shape particles into the enemy silhouette.
  • Added noise, velocity variation, animated lifetime values, and forces to create a dissolve-and-reform effect.
  • Designed the effect to make the enemy feel unstable and unpredictable in dark environments.
